PaidVault Support

Need help or have a suggestion? Email UsefulApps.help@icloud.com. If something isn't working, it helps to attach the log file: in the app, go to About, then Share Log File.

My policies aren't syncing

Check that you're signed in to iCloud on each device. Sync can take a few moments, so try reopening the app.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for PaidVault in Settings, and that reminders are on for that policy. On the About screen, Send Test Notification checks that notifications can reach you.

How do I back up my data?

In the app, go to About, then Export & Import.

How do I share with a family member?

Tap the people icon on the main screen and send them the invitation. They'll need the app and an iCloud account.
