PaidVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

We don't collect your data

We don't run servers for the app, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We can't see anything you enter.

What the app stores

The details you enter, such as policies, due dates, amounts, notes and links, are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they also sync through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. An email address you save for email reminders stays on your device.

What you can choose to share

  • If you invite someone to share your policies, they can see that information through iCloud, and you can remove their access at any time.
  • If you create an email reminder, it opens in your mail app, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • If you export a backup, you decide where that file goes. It isn't encrypted by the app, so store it somewhere safe.
  • The app keeps a log file on your device for troubleshooting. It records technical details about sync and notifications, and may include error messages and sharing links. It only leaves your device if you choose to send it.
  • If you email us for support, we receive whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to help you.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting a policy removes it from the app and from your iCloud sync. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. You can manage or remove iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
